Hello!

My game is called 'Light Rider'. 

*Inspiration*: The 1980's Tron Light Racer game by Brian Bates inspired the neon colours and the idea of following tracks of light.

*Link to the theme of 'bounce back'*: Straight tracks (depending on how straight) makes your object bounce in a trajectory perpendicular to the straight track its just bounced off of.

*How to play*: Click and drag to draw a track of blue light that your orange object will follow. Straight lines add chaos by making your object bounce. Try to beat your highest score for going the
furthest distance to the '[mystery direction]' as you are pulled down by gravity. Get stuck and you can try to get yourself out but if you aren't successful then you will end up restarting with all 
of your lines from the previous round acting as obstacles. 

Have fun!
